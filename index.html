01 // SPATIAL LINEAGE

Ethiopian Spatial Archetypes & Modern BIM

We reject the placeless glass box. Our architecture is grounded in indigenous Ethiopian building sciences, re-coded through computational Building Information Modeling (BIM) and environmental simulations.

ARCHETYPE 01 1ST–10TH CENTURY

Aksumite Timber-Stone Joinery

Re'ese Anbasā (Monkey Heads) & Stele Monumentality

Alternating layers of dressed stone with projecting squared wooden tie-beams created earthquake-resilient monumental structures. We translate this into modern parametric hybrid timber-concrete facade framing and thermal isolation assemblies.

MODERN BIM APPLICATION: LOD 350 Seismic Facade Joinery
ARCHETYPE 02 12TH–13TH CENTURY

Lalibela Subtractive Monoliths

Carved Volcanic Tuff & 'Tena' Daylight Shafts

Monolithic churches carved top-down out of red volcanic stone demonstrate supreme mastery of thermal massing, subterranean cooling trenches, and dramatic vertical light well shafts that illuminate sacred interiors without solar heat gain.

MODERN BIM APPLICATION: Passive Geothermal & Daylight Wells
ARCHETYPE 03 16TH CENTURY

Harari Nadaba Spatial Hierarchy

Five-Tier Seating & Wall Niche Geometries

The traditional Harari *Gē Gār* organizes social rituals through multi-level raised plaster seating platforms (*Nadaba*) and recessed display niches (*Tāqēt*). We synthesize this for luxury residential salons, sunken lounges, and custom acoustic millwork.

MODERN BIM APPLICATION: Custom Interior Millwork & Sunken Lounges
ARCHETYPE 04 INDIGENOUS VERNACULAR

Sidama Bamboo Hyperboloids

Radial Bamboo Arches & Breathable Envelopes

Woven highland bamboo structures with self-ventilating thatch peaks and natural parabolic curve load distribution. We integrate this structural intelligence into parametric timber gridshells, solar shading pavilions, and educational campuses.

MODERN BIM APPLICATION: Parametric Timber Gridshells & Canopies
